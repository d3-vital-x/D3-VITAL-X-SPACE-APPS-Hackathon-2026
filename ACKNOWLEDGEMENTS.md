## 🔐 Data Integrity, Provenance & Responsible Research

The project follows a number of research-engineering principles:

-   preserve raw inputs whenever possible
-   calculate integrity hashes for uploaded data
-   maintain provenance metadata
-   distinguish measured results from computational observations
-   distinguish observations from hypotheses
-   require human review for research-oriented anomaly interpretation
-   avoid unsupported clinical or operational claims
-   keep proprietary research-core implementation separate from public
    interfaces

The project's internal claim-classification approach distinguishes:

-   **C1 --- Measured / directly supported**
-   **C2 --- Computational observation**
-   **C3 --- Hypothesis / future research direction**

This classification is intended to reduce overstatement and improve
reviewer-facing clarity.

## 🧭 Research Philosophy

D³ VITAL-X is built around a simple principle:

> **One AI Engine. Multiple Worlds. One Signal Language.**

The platform seeks to provide a common technical workflow for
heterogeneous scientific inputs while keeping scientific interpretation
separate from automated computation.

The project emphasizes:

**Data → Integrity → Quality → Features → Analytics → Evidence → Human
Review**

rather than treating an automated score as a scientific conclusion.
